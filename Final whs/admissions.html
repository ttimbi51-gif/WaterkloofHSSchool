Admission Requirements for Waterkloof Hills Secondary School

  • Completed application form
  • Certified copy of birth certificate
  • Certified copy of parent/guardian ID
  • Latest school report (previous year)
  • Transfer letter from previous school (if applicable)
  • Proof of residence (utility bill or lease)
  • 2 passport-size photos of the learner
  • Immunisation / clinic card

Important Dates

  • Applications open: 1 July 2026
  • Applications close: 30 September 2026
  • Results communicated: November 2026
  • Registration of accepted learners: January 2027
