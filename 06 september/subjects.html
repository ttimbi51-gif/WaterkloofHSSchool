Subject Selection Requirements

  • Learners must choose a minimum of 7 subjects (3 compulsory + 4 electives)
  • At least one language must be at Home Language level
  • Subject changes may only be made within the first two weeks of Term 1
  • Certain university programmes require specific subjects — consult your teacher for guidance
